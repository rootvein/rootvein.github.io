anomalies can be only seen through security cameras installed throughout the school. 00A appears to be way less hostile when accompanied with beings that have the ability to speak
with them. 00A also appears to be developing a strong bond with 00B the more they talk to them.

00B appears to have a full pitch black body with a glowing red face. It has 5 sticks coming out from its back that have red glowing edges. Unlike 00A, 00B has the ability to roam
around freely with no restrictions. Further researched must be needed.
